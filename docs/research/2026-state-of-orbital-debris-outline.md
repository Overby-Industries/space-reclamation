# "State of Orbital Debris & Solutions 2026" — Outline

Skeleton for the Phase 1 deliverable named in the [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md).
This is an outline, not a draft — the actual figures, findings, and
citations get filled in as Phase 1 research happens, not fabricated ahead
of it.

1. **Executive Summary**
   - Scope of the report, intended audience (investors, regulators, the
     public).
2. **Current State of the Orbital Debris Environment**
   - Population by size class and orbital regime (source: DISCOS, ESA
     Space Environment Report — see `sources.md`).
   - Growth trend and major fragmentation events since the last published
     report.
3. **Regulatory Landscape**
   - COPUOS guidelines, ODMSP, and any newly adopted national/international
     rules since the prior year.
4. **Capture Technology Survey**
   - Summary of `capture-technologies.md`, updated with any new flight
     demonstrations.
5. **Overby's Position**
   - Where the reclaim-and-repurpose model sits relative to
     deorbit-only approaches, referencing the open questions in
     `capture-technologies.md` and how they were resolved (or weren't).
6. **Appendix: Full Source List**
   - Everything in `sources.md`, in citation format.
