# Sources

Starter bibliography for the Phase 1 knowledge-consolidation effort. Add to
this list rather than duplicating citations elsewhere in the org's repos —
see [`README.md`](README.md).

## Statistics & Tracking

| Source | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ESA Space Environment Report (annual) | European Space Agency | Tracks debris population, fragmentation events, and reentry statistics year over year. |
| NASA Orbital Debris Quarterly News | NASA Orbital Debris Program Office (ODPO) | Ongoing bulletin on debris events, conjunction assessments, and mitigation research. |
| DISCOS (Database and Information System Characterising Objects in Space) | ESA Space Debris Office | Catalog of tracked objects; reference for object-count and size-distribution claims. |

## Regulatory & Policy

| Source | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Space Debris Mitigation Guidelines | UN Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS) | Baseline international guidelines most national mitigation policy references. |
| Orbital Debris Mitigation Standard Practices (ODMSP) | US Government (NASA/FCC/FAA) | US domestic standard; relevant for any US-licensed mission. |

## Capture Technology (Active Debris Removal)

| Source | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| RemoveDEBRIS mission results | Surrey Space Centre / Airbus | In-orbit demonstration of net and harpoon capture methods. |
| e.Deorbit study | ESA | Feasibility study comparing capture methods (robotic arm, net, tether) for a large derelict target. |
| Electrodynamic tether deorbit studies | NASA / academic literature | Passive deorbit approach using Earth's magnetic field, no propellant. |

## Asteroid-Adjacent Precedent (Containment & Capture)

| Source | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Asteroid Redirect Mission (ARM) concept studies | NASA | Studied "bagging" a small asteroid/boulder for containment and capture — the closest existing precedent to Overby's dustless-containment mining concept, cited in the org README's GPT-5 analysis. |
