# Capture Technology Comparison

Known active-debris-removal (ADR) capture approaches, for evaluating which
fit Overby's reclaim-and-repurpose model (capture → process → reuse) rather
than a capture-and-deorbit-only model. See [`sources.md`](sources.md) for
citations.

| Approach | Maturity (as of source material) | Fits reclaim-and-repurpose? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net capture | Flight-demonstrated (RemoveDEBRIS) | Yes, if paired with a containment/processing pod | Good for irregular/tumbling objects; net itself becomes part of the captured mass unless recovered. |
| Harpoon capture | Flight-demonstrated (RemoveDEBRIS) | Partial | Effective for controlled targets with known structure; less suited to fragment-scale debris relevant to Overby's "dustless" containment concept. |
| Robotic arm capture | Studied (e.Deorbit) | Yes | Best precision for intact derelict capture ahead of processing; highest cost/complexity of the options here. |
| Tether/harpoon-tether hybrid | Studied (e.Deorbit) | Partial | Primarily a deorbit mechanism, not a containment-for-reuse mechanism as-is. |
| Electrodynamic tether | Studied, some flight tests | No (deorbit-only) | Passive, propellant-free deorbit; doesn't capture material for reuse, relevant mainly for end-of-life disposal of Overby's own assets rather than third-party debris reclamation. |
| Magnetic/electrostatic capture | Conceptual | Yes, for ferrous fragments specifically | Would need a dedicated non-contact stage ahead of any processing pod; only applies to ferromagnetic debris. |
| Containment bag capture | Studied (NASA ARM concept, non-debris context) | Yes — closest existing precedent | The org's mining-concept "dustless containment bags" are the same underlying idea applied to reclamation; see the ARM citation in `sources.md`. |

## Open questions for Phase 1

- Which of the above (if any) has been demonstrated at a size/mass class
  relevant to Overby's target debris population, vs. only demonstrated on
  a single controlled test article?
- What's the actual cost-per-kg-captured for each, and how does that
  compare to the "Space Cleanup as a Service" business model in the
  [README](../../README.md)?
- Containment-bag capture is the best conceptual fit but has the least
  flight heritage of the options above — what's the gap between the ARM
  studies and an actual debris-capture demonstration?

These are explicitly unanswered — filling them in with real findings is
Phase 1 work, not something to guess at here.
